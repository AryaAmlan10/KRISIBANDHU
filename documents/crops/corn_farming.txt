CORN FARMING GUIDE

Crop Name: Corn (Maize)

Climate:
Corn grows well in warm weather with adequate sunlight.

Soil:
Well-drained, fertile loamy soil is suitable.

Land Preparation:
Prepare the field by removing weeds and making the soil loose.

Sowing:
Use healthy, certified maize seeds. Follow local agricultural
recommendations for spacing and sowing depth.

Water Management:
Provide adequate moisture, especially during germination,
flowering and grain development. Avoid waterlogging.

Weed Management:
Keep the field free from weeds during early crop growth.

Harvesting:
Harvest when the cobs and grains reach maturity.
